[h1]1.1.3 — The dead are outside magic's reach[/h1]

The patch already stopped Supernatural's magic from landing on a wight or an Other. This closes the ways round the back, and settles what happens when the two mods reach for the same character at the same moment.

[h2]Reworks[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]The species gate now recognises every one of the dead, not just the ones wearing the trait.[/b] It checked four traits. A lord the Others convert where he stands keeps his own body and his own dynasty — what marks him is the government he now runs and the stamp on his name, and he was passing every gate in the mod. He no longer does. One change, and it re-answers the question at all 77 places Supernatural asks it.
[*][b]The dead do not get a second chance at death.[/b] Vanilla's Know Thyself, vampire blood in the system and R'hllor's last kiss all argue over the same rule, and none of the three was written with corpses in mind. A man who drank vampire blood, died, and was raised carried that blood into the horde — he would spend the second chance, be refused at the species gate, and simply not die. R'hllor does not bargain with the Night King for one of his, either.
[*][b]Being raised now takes the blood with the rest of it.[/b] The raise already unmakes a vampire, werewolf, hybrid or hunter. It now also clears vampire blood a mortal drank before he fell, rather than leaving a corpse holding a ticket nobody can cash.
[*][b]You cannot teach witchcraft to a corpse.[/b] The conversion scheme kept dragons out and had nothing to say about wights. A scheme already in flight now collapses if the dead take the target first — there is no one left in there to convert.
[*][b]Nor can the AI make one of them a witch behind your back.[/b] Becoming a witch has about a hundred entry points and only the ones you click were checked — the place-of-power sweep, the magic casus belli and the court-position hook all walked past. All of them now ask the same question. (Needs Supernatural 2.31c.)
[*][b]And no drinking from the dead.[/b] "The Drunk's Ramblings" picked any adult in your court to feed on, which in a Long Night playset could hand you a wight. It picks someone with blood in them now. (Needs Supernatural 2.31c.)
[*][b]And the same for eight more paths the AI walks on its own.[/b] The yearly trait sweep, the witchcraft dynasty legacy, a mythical legend's reward, a court mage choosing his own curse victim, a cult priest appointment, the place-of-power sweep, the werewolf bite chain, and a holy war whose victory converted an entire faith at once. None of them asked whether the target was a person. (Needs Supernatural 2.31c.)
[/list]

[h2]Combat: who owns the blow[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Dragonriders keep AGOT's rules.[/b] Supernatural 2.33 catches a mortal blow for anyone carrying vampire blood, using the same "this must happen" weight AGOT uses to put a dragon into a battle. Two of those in one pool and one of them silently loses. Supernatural stands down for a rider in the air, and catches him on foot like everyone else.
[*][b]The blood goes before the fire.[/b] A blessed champion carrying vampire blood is wanted by both mods at the same instant. The blood is a single cup and it is spent on the first fall; R'hllor's promise is renewable and is not spent by it. So the blood takes that fall, Supernatural leaves the pool for good, and the next time he goes down the fire has it to itself. Both systems, in sequence, instead of the newer one quietly eating the older.
[/list]

[h2]Bug Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Fixed the Mikaelsons' portraits under AGOT 0.5.1.[/b] AGOT added ten genes this patch's DNA entries did not carry. They do now. Pairs with the beard repair in Supernatural 2.31c — both are needed for it to reach you.
[/list]
