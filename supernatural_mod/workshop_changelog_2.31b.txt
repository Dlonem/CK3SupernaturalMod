[h1]2.31b[/h1]

A compatibility and bug-hunting pass. Save-safe — no new game needed.

[h2]Playing alongside A Game of Thrones[/h2]

Supernatural was written for a world where every character is a person. Loaded next to a total conversion that is no longer true, so the mod now asks first.

[list]
[*][b]Dragons cannot be turned, compelled, dominated, cursed or healed.[/b] They are not people. Every magic and creature interaction now checks, and says so in the tooltip instead of failing silently.
[*][b]The dead are excluded too.[/b] Wights, Others and the Night King, when The Long Night is installed. A corpse with somebody else's will in it has nothing for a spell to take hold of.
[*]This costs nothing in a normal game. Without a total conversion loaded, every character is still a valid target, exactly as before.
[/list]

[h2]Fixes[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Spymasters can uncover every secret type again.[/b] The mod was replacing the base game's list of fourteen with its own four, and disabling the fallback — so a spymaster on Find Secrets could only ever turn up supernatural ones. All eighteen are back, and the fallback works.
[*][b]Perk point alerts work for the four creature focuses.[/b] Vampires, werewolves, hybrids and hunters were never told they had a point to spend.
[*][b]130 spell tooltips showed a raw key instead of a sentence.[/b] Things like "spn_cannot_be_turned_custom has no localization" in place of the reason. Every one of them reads properly now, in all supported languages.
[*][b]The Magic Council's summoned host arrives at full strength.[/b] Three of its five regiment types did not exist, so most of the army never showed up.
[*][b]Dark temples and magic academies no longer error for landless casters.[/b]
[*][b]The eight Originals use the current portrait gene set.[/b] Their DNA predated several gene additions, so their portraits were being assembled from an incomplete description.
[*][b]Supernaturals keep modern hair.[/b] The mod carried an old copy of the base game's hair rules, which quietly reverted every character to a much older set. Rebuilt against the current game — and supernaturals still never go bald.
[/list]

[h2]Changes[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Yearly NPC trait sweeps no longer walk every character at once.[/b] Six separate passes over the entire world every 1 January, replaced with one check per character spread across the year. Same result, a fraction of the cost.
[*]A duplicate on_action warning at load is gone.
[/list]
