[h1]2.34a — The people who made you know what you are[/h1]

A hotfix for one bug. No new content.

[h2]Bug Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Your spymaster and the scrying orb stop "finding" secrets you already know.[/b] Find Secrets and scrying both pick a secret in your court that the game says you have not learned, and the base game leans toward family. Supernatural was creating its secrets with nobody recorded as knowing them — a child born to two werewolves came of age with a secret his parents were never told; a vampire you embraced in an event carried a secret the game did not credit you with; a hunter you trained, the same. So the werewolf father kept being told his werewolf son is a werewolf. Now, the moment one of the four supernatural secrets is created, the people who made that character are told: the maker, the sire whose blood it was, the trainer, and a living supernatural parent for the born. Every road into a creature trait goes through the same line, so no path is missed.
[*][b]Running games are repaired once.[/b] The first time a save is loaded under 2.34a, every existing supernatural secret is shown to the same people. Nothing else about your save changes.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Safe to drop onto a running save.
[*]Reported by Mertehan. This is separate from the 2.31c fix that let spymasters uncover every secret type again.
[/list]
