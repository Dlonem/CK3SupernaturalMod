[h1]Supernatural[/h1]

Most mods that make you a vampire hand you a trait and a stat bonus, and that is the end of it.

This one gives you a secret to keep, a hunger you have to answer, a maker you did not choose, and a hunter somewhere in the world who has started asking questions about your court.

Vampires, werewolves, hybrids, hunters, witches and demons for CK3. Runs on its own. Works with A Game of Thrones through a patch.

[img]https://imgur.com/U4fbA7p[/img]

[list]
[*][b]What you are and what they know are two different traits.[/b] Every creature carries a hidden one and a public one. Being exposed is what changes how the world treats you, and under the wrong faith it is a crime.
[*][b]Six lifestyles, ten perk trees.[/b] Vampire, werewolf, hybrid, hunter, witch and demon, each with its own focuses, progression tracks and a capstone.
[*][b]You turn people, and the game remembers who.[/b] Maker and Progeny are real relations written on both characters, by every route in — bitten, asked, forced, inherited, or bled out on a battlefield.
[*][b]Feeding costs a city something.[/b] A clean feed leaves the county poorer and harder to hold, and a drained county is easier to feed in next time. Vampires make the world worse on purpose.
[*][b]Seven regiments you will not find anywhere else[/b] — Vampires, Werewolves and Bats, plus Cultists, Skeleton Warriors, Demonic Knights and Battle Golems from the magic side.
[*][b]The AI plays all of it.[/b] Lords turn each other, hunters take apprentices, witches build covens, and personality decides who does what. A content lord does not go looking for blood.
[*][b]Twenty-one game rules[/b], eighteen loading screens, and a main menu in the right colour.
[/list]

[img]https://imgur.com/qffe4Fo[/img]

[b]Feeding is a journey, not a button.[/b] Travel to a city — yours or somebody else's — and hold a feeding there. Up to five guests, all of whom must be vampires or hybrids; everyone else in the room is a local. You choose one person and work them across the whole activity: scout the place, lay a trap, arm-wrestle a man in a tavern, and then decide what you are actually going to do with him.

[b]Fall in battle with vampire blood in you and you do not die.[/b] You wake three days later understanding nothing. A fortnight on the hunger arrives, and with it the memory of a cup somebody handed you months ago. Feed on a dying captain, ask the one who gave it to you, or refuse. Refuse twice and you die of it.

[b]Compulsion.[/b] Look a mortal in the eye and tell them what to think. It does not work on witches, on demons, or on anything with blood like yours.

[b]Fledgling, Vampire, Revered, Elder[/b], and True Vampire at the end of the tree. Fangs and pallor are real portrait changes, with rules to turn either off.

[img]https://imgur.com/sZjQJTW[/img]

[b]The bite is a disease, not a switch.[/b] Claws leave a wound that festers for one to three years — Festering, Virulent, Ravaging, Consuming — and at the end of it the change either takes hold or it kills you. In between: fevered nights, a healer who asks the wrong question, a hunter who starts paying attention, and the wolf who clawed you turning up to see how you are getting on.

[b]It runs in the blood.[/b] Lycanthropy is genetic and it inherits, and the rule governing how has eight settings. Children do not advertise it, and working out who in your line carries it is meant to take attention.

[b]Fledgling, Werewolf, Feral, Dread, Primal.[/b] The wolf tree is deliberately cheaper and quicker than the vampire's, because a werewolf is not immortal and has one lifetime to finish it in.

[img]https://imgur.com/Sz3eb6W[/img]

[b]Nobody is born one.[/b] There is no hybrid in the world at the start, and none will ever be born. The only way to become one is for a hybrid to make you, so the first one in your game is a decision somebody made.

[b]Both halves, and both prices.[/b] Two perk trees and two progression tracks, the vampire's and the wolf's, and the longest climb in the mod. A known hybrid frightens a court more than a known vampire does.

[img]https://imgur.com/Hjkc9RJ[/img]

[b]Hunters are trained, not born.[/b] Find someone who knows the work and ask, or take an apprentice yourself and put them through it. Training can go wrong, and an apprentice who gets bitten finishes what is in his blood before he finishes his training.

[b]The work comes to you.[/b] A monster is sighted. A witch is spotted in the next valley. You go, and you take it down, or it gets away, or it infects you, or it kills you.

[b]And it comes the other way.[/b] Hunters come for creatures — in a garden, in a corridor, in your own bed at three in the morning. Nine ways that ends, and one of them is the creature killing the hunter with nothing but the bedding.

[b]Landless works.[/b] An adventurer can take a Witch Hunt contract off the board and make a living at it.

[img]https://imgur.com/wclSZZp[/img]

[b]The magic half is here too.[/b] Witches, warlocks, archmages and covens; spells learned one at a time; a high council to be invited onto or thrown off; a mages' summit, an arena, and endgame crises that arrive whether or not you were paying attention.

[b]Demons.[/b] Summon one, become one, or have children with one — demonic inheritance is its own rule with four settings, and its own sixteen-perk tree.

[b]Artifacts worth going after.[/b] The Ring Of Everlasting, the Black Grimoire, the Magic Catalyst and more besides. Some are found. Some have to be made.

[img]https://imgur.com/eqbt766[/img]

Twenty-one of them, and they go further than on and off.

Whether the world seeds creatures at all, and down to which rank of ruler. Whether the AI may turn people, and which species may. Whether turning someone can kill them, and how often. How supernatural blood inherits — eight settings, from strictly like-with-like to not at all — with demons on four of their own. Whether a vampire's portrait ages, freezes, or stops at maturity. Fangs. Pallor.

[b]It is meant to be your world.[/b] Play a hunter in a realm where nothing supernatural exists except you and the thing you are chasing. Or turn all of it on and see who is left.

[b]Do not run this alongside Witchcraft or V&L.[/b] Supernatural already carries that content; loading either next to it puts two copies of the same files against each other. Also known not to get along: AGOT: Battlefield Duels, Better Barbershop, Asia Expansion Project, Cultural Armies Pack.

The magic half of this mod is [b]Rycie's[/b] Witchcraft, carried forward here with their updates merged in as they ship. [b]Nope's[/b] V&L is the same lineage. Neither belongs in a playset with this one. Thanks also to the [b]Princes of Darkness[/b] team, BDW and Sparc especially, and to [b]Luke0704[/b] for the Spanish translation.
