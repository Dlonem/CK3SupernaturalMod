# (spn_hide_make_champion_effect), and removed only when the hide truly leaves him (spn_hide_exit_effect). Taking the

# The Hide Bond track is shared by key: track names only need to be unique within one trait (_traits.info:198).
# Track tiers are cumulative, like lycanthropy's. Icons are tinted stand-ins of true_werewolf.dds until art exists.
