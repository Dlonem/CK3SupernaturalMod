Version 2.28 (06/24/2026)  [Quality-Control & Bug-Fix Pass]
	NEW:
		+ Custom Supernatural loading-screen tips now rotate in alongside the vanilla ones (Vampire, Werewolf, Hybrid, Hunter, Vampire Feeding, Sire/Progeny bonds, and the supernatural men-at-arms). Added as a separate file, so vanilla loading tips are left fully intact.
		+ New "Vampire Feeding" game concept (a blue hover-link) explaining how a feeding works - including that a clean, undiscovered feeding quietly lowers the host county's Development and Control.

	FIXES:
		+ Vampire lifestyle perk tree: Master of Shadows and Vampire Nest could not be hovered or selected (they sat outside the tree's clickable area). Rebuilt the Vampirism tree as a symmetric, bottom-heavy "drop of blood" layout that stays within selectable bounds - all ten perks now work and the bottom-row labels no longer overlap.
		+ Compulsion now notifies both sides: a successful compulsion and a resisted one each send a message to the vampire and the mortal (the events existed but were never fired). A compelled mortal now learns why they suddenly owe a hook or acted against themselves, and a mortal who resists realizes what you are.
		+ Cleared a large block of faith-persecution load errors: the three overridden faith checks (shunned / criminal / negative-attitude) were rewritten from a flag-token pattern to direct trait comparison. No behavior change - witches, vampires, werewolves, hybrids and the usual vanilla traits are still shunned/criminal exactly as before - and this also fixes a latent case where exposing a known supernatural failed to remove piety.
		+ Psychic Surgery spell no longer errors: two leftover references to the retired witch_hunter2 trait were swapped to supernatural_hunter (matching the established convention).
		+ Court-position tooltip fixed: a reference to the removed witch_hunter2 trait was repointed to supernatural_hunter so it no longer fails to display.
		+ Corrected the error-suppression event: it was manufacturing several of its own "variable used but never set" warnings (referencing traits as if they were variables) and had lost its required UTF-8-BOM encoding; cleaned up, re-encoded, and the Mikaelson flag plus werewolf-bite scaffolding values are now properly accounted for.

	NOTES:
		+ Remaining load-log entries are vanilla/DLC (e.g. the countermeasure_parameter scheme trigger) or belong to the Witchcraft addon (no_soul, mage_defeated_list, special_magic_guardian, MC_FUNDS_DESC) - none are core Supernatural.
		+ Still on the list for a later pass: latent witch_hunter2 references in the non-English localizations + a court-position desc; and a closer playtest of the "known vampire" faith reactions.

Version 2.27 (06/19/2026)  [Werewolf Turning Overhaul - Phase 2]
	CHANGES:
		+ Reworked the werewolf turning around a claw-wound theme (reserving "bite" for vampires down the road): the interactions are now Offer Gift / Force Gift / Ask for the Gift, and the affliction is the Infected Wound trait. The lineage relation reads "Clawed By".
		+ New incubation event "A Crisis of Faith": a one-shot reckoning with your faith over what you are becoming, with branching paths for the zealous, the cynical, the devout, and the learned (piety, stress, and infection consequences that feed the yield/resist spine)
		+ "The One Who Clawed You" gains a third path - take the sire's help now and settle the debt later (a forced turning can sour into rivalry even as they guide you through it)
		+ "The Wound Worsens" gains a Learned/Physician path to treat the rot yourself, with better odds than a desperate fight
		+ Expanded the role-play text in several incubation event descriptions (the festering wound, the healer's vigil, and more)

	BALANCING:
		+ Offer Gift acceptance reworked to feel personal rather than transactional: lower base, and a layered, organic set of reasons - boldness, ambition, a death already at the door (illness/grievous wounds), despair, devotion, dread, and faith - instead of a flat "do I like you" check. Rank and politics deliberately play no part.
		+ Offer Gift is now moderately harder to land: the bold, the ambitious, the dying, and those who love or fear you still accept; idle, content, or craven strangers will not
		+ Acceptance breakdowns now show the numeric value of every reason, not just the text
		+ Ask for the Gift acceptance rebuilt around what actually moves a werewolf to spread the curse (kinship, trust, faith, pity for the dying, ambition for a pack), and stripped of the inherited vassalization logic (faith pluralism, culture group, illegitimacy) that never belonged here
		+ Force Gift can now be used on neighbouring rulers (your realm + the realms bordering it), not only those already in your court or grasp
		+ Voluntary turnings now begin the infection at a randomized 5-15 (was a flat 10); the wound's passive worsening is a steady +3 per tick
		+ The Infected Wound trait now disables combat leadership while it festers

	FIXES:
		+ Fixed an invalid trigger (is_liege_of) and a misplaced disables_combat_leadership token in the wound trait that were throwing errors on load

	NEW (modding hooks):
		+ Added the 'spn_event_bonus_to_gift_accept' character modifier - a ready hook for future "temptation"/sway events to prime a character toward accepting the Gift (read by Offer Gift acceptance, +25)
		+ Added the 'spn_desperate_for_a_cure_trigger' scripted trigger (illness / grievous wounds / wasting conditions), shared by the gift interactions

	NOTES:
		+ A lover/spouse "you seem different" post-turn event is planned for the next phase

Supernatural – Witchcraft Addon (Update X.X) — Changelog
New

“Invoke the Bond” (Vampire ⇄ Progeny Interaction)
Right-click your Progeny and Invoke the Bond to compel specific actions. Your Vampirism/Hybrid Vampirism XP fuels each order (cost varies by command). Stronger sire ⇒ more reliable outcomes.

Examples: demand gold, call them into your war, claim titles, reveal secrets, convert faith, “join my court,” make them a lover, even catastrophic orders (very costly).

Works for Vampires and Hybrids; ignores Demons (lore-friendly immunity).

Clear tooltips explain requirements and XP costs. Options grey out when you lack juice.

Astaroth Assassination – Supernatural Escapes
During the Apocalypse chain, Vampires/Hybrids/Werewolves/Hunters now get themed escape options (with sensible XP or skill checks) to avoid the old “auto-death” feel. If you make it out, you’ll land in a clean follow-up scene instead of the execution path.

Monster Hunter Template
New Supernatural Monster Hunter template that can spawn via event/effect, complete with the proper Hunter secret/trait assignment on creation.

Changed

Interaction Category (UI Quality-of-Life)
Repurposed the base-game Favorites interaction category into “Supernatural.”

Favoriting a Supernatural interaction now helps surface it together under this label.

(If you favorite an interaction, you may see it listed twice under Supernatural—by design of how favorites render.)

Game Concepts & Tooltips
Added/expanded Sire and Progeny concepts with concise, in-world descriptions so players instantly understand the bond and its influence. Also refreshed Vampire/Werewolf/Hybrid concepts for consistency.

Balance

Bond Costs Scale with Power
Each order under Invoke the Bond consumes a small to major chunk of Vampirism/Hybrid-Vampirism XP (e.g., light nudges ~5–15, heavy domination up to very high). Costs and gating lean on your current track XP, not hooks, so playstyles that invest in vampiric growth feel tangibly stronger.

Sire vs. Progeny Margin (Under the Hood)
A compact scripted value compares sire power vs. progeny power for AI acceptance and subtle modifiers—keeps things flavorful without micro-math for players.

Fixes & Polish

Robust valid/invalid messaging for bond options (why you can’t use it, what you’re missing).

Cleaned portraits/scenes in the escape follow-ups so Astaroth doesn’t “teleport” into your getaway.

New opinion modifier for using the bond (for when things… strain the relationship).
