[h1]Hotfix 2.31a[/h1]

A disease-immunity fix on top of 2.31.

[list]
[*][b]Vampires, werewolves and hybrids are properly immune to disease.[/b] The immunity was only ever applied on some of the ways you can be turned, so a lot of supernaturals never had it — and hybrids had it removed outright by the transformation, leaving them worse off than the werewolf they used to be. A hybrid could catch consumption.
[*][b]Consumption is cured when you turn,[/b] like every other illness. It was the only disease missing from that list.
[*][b]Existing characters are repaired automatically.[/b] No new game needed — anyone already turned gets their immunity back on the next yearly tick.
[/list]

Nothing else in 2.31 has changed.
