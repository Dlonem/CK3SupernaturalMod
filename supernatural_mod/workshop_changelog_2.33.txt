[h1]2.33 — The Blood Remembers[/h1]

Fall in battle with a vampire's blood in you and you do not die. You wake three days later on the wrong side of it, and you have about a month to decide what you are.

[h2]New: dying in battle with vampire blood in your system[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]The blood catches the blow that would have killed you.[/b] If a mortal carrying vampire blood takes a fatal hit as a knight, he goes down, comes off the field, and wakes up three days later — no pain, no heartbeat, and a wound that has closed over clean.
[*][b]Three events, and you are not told what is happening.[/b] You wake first and understand nothing. A fortnight later the hunger arrives, and with it the memory of a cup somebody handed you months ago and made very little of at the time.
[*][b]Feed, ask, or refuse.[/b] Drain a dying captain, a knight still standing, or a stranger on the field. If the one who gave you the blood is there, ask them instead and nobody has to die. Or refuse, and get one more fortnight to change your mind before it is decided for you.
[*][b]Refuse twice and you die of it[/b] — a new death reason, "Refused the Blood."
[*][b]Whoever gave you the blood becomes your Maker.[/b] Full Maker and Progeny relation, not just a note in a tooltip.
[*][b]The AI weighs this like a person would.[/b] A twenty-year-old with his whole life ahead of him takes the blood far more often than a man of seventy. Stubborn and zealous characters refuse; the craven and the gluttonous do not. Having small children pushes hard toward living.
[*][b]Works landed or landless, and needs no compatibility patch.[/b]
[/list]

[h2]Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Maker and Progeny are now set on every way you turn.[/b] The relation only ever existed if a vampire turned you through the interaction. Dying with blood in your system, asking to be turned, working out what was in you, or experimenting with it all made a vampire with no Maker at all. Five paths, one fix.
[*][b]Supernatural's men-at-arms no longer throw errors when a war ends.[/b] All four types asked whether the recruiter was a werewolf or a vampire in a context where there is no character to ask about.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Dragonriders are left to A Game of Thrones.[/b] A rider in the air is resolved by their rules; the blood catches him on foot like everyone else.
[*][b]The blood goes before the fire.[/b] If you are blessed by R'hllor and carrying vampire blood, the blood takes the fall first and is spent. Your blessing is untouched and catches the next one. Both systems, in order, instead of one silently eating the other.
[/list]
